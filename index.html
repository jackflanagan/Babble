On touch screens the on-screen buttons always drive Player 1.

Play together from two separate phones or computers, each on its own screen. Both of you need to open this exact page — one person hosts, the other joins with the code. This only works once the game is hosted on the open web (not from this in-chat preview), and both players need an internet connection.

Create a room, then send the code to your friend however you like (text, chat, out loud).

Enter the code your friend sent you.

Your own controls always move your fox: A/D or the side buttons to move, W/↑ or the up button to jump, Shift or the round button to blow a bubble.

Quick tip: Shoot bubbles to trap enemies, then bump the bubble to pop it! Grab collectibles for bonus points. Power-ups drop from popped enemies — ⚡ speed, 🔁 rapid fire, 🛡 shield. Good luck!
