Privacy

Last updated: 2026-09-22

What Babble itself stores

Babble is a client-side game — everything below lives only in your browser's localStorage, on your own device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no Babble-run server or analytics.

  • Your progress, best scores and mastery stars for each location
  • A display name, only if you've typed one in to appear on a leaderboard
  • Unlocked skins/cosmetics
  • Preferences: volume, whether you've seen the tutorial, daily-challenge and streak status, achievements

None of this is sent anywhere by Babble itself. Clearing your browser data, or using the in-game Reset button, removes all of it.

The platform you're playing on

If you're playing Babble on CrazyGames or Poki, that platform's own SDK may show ads and collect data about your visit, per its own privacy policy (linked above). Babble doesn't control what those platforms collect — please check their policies directly for specifics.

Optional online features

Leaderboard: if a leaderboard is configured, submitting a score sends your typed display name and score to a database Babble uses for that purpose. No other personal data is collected by it.

"Two phones" multiplayer: this connects two browsers directly to each other (peer-to-peer, no game server in between) using WebRTC. Setting up that direct connection relies on a public signaling/tracker service, which — as an inherent part of how WebRTC connections are established — can see the IP addresses of the two browsers while they're pairing. This isn't something Babble adds or controls; it's how the underlying technology works.
